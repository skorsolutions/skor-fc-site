# Planned substitution waves — v58.27

In Captain Portal → Lineups → Planned Substitutions, choose Players Out from the field and Players In from the bench before that wave. Wave 1 uses the starting lineup. Following waves project each complete valid swap in saved list order, carrying first-half changes into the second half. Exact minutes and cadence controls retain their existing behavior; list order determines planning eligibility.

Original starters have gold chips and a visible Started label, including on the projected bench and when selected to return. An original bench player retains ordinary styling after coming off. Starter identity is derived from the saved starting field, not stored separately. Planning does not change actual attendance, official events or the starting field itself.

For example, Tom starts and Alex is on the bench. In Wave 1, Tom comes out and Alex goes in. Tom now appears on the bench in gold. Wave 2 offers Tom under Players In and Alex under Players Out. A match guest/TEMP player works through the same stable-key flow even without a known jersey number.

Incomplete or invalid waves keep their selected chips and show a review message. Only equal-count swaps of distinct eligible players affect the projected lineup. Later waves use the last valid lineup and warn about earlier problems. Changing the lineup or moving/removing a wave recalculates every dependent choice. Existing invalid chips can always be deselected; stale old buttons cannot add newly ineligible players. Explicit Absent/player removal and Clear Pitch still remove their relevant references.

Save unfinished plans as a non-Final variation/Tinker draft. Correct the waves before marking Production / Final, publishing the current board, overwriting an existing Final or downloading the gameplan PNG/JPG. The first-half coverage reminder counts complete valid entries only. No arbitrary player replacement or silent wave repair occurs. Old saved scalar first/second/halftime plans normalize through the existing editor flow.

`sub-wave-plan.js` is a pure projection and eligibility helper. The editor uses normalized state, stable regular/TEMP IDs, the saved standard slots or Freeform player keys and the starting bench. Original `state.subs` arrays, wave labels/groups/minutes, JSON browser drafts, shared variation saves/copies and Final publication payloads remain the same. Saved snapshots for another game are validated independently of the editor's current match-specific roster. Valid waves continue into the existing Game Day, cadence/alarm, gameplan renderer and permitted AI context. Referee cards, keeper pairs/number hierarchy, Strategy, Player Portal and public/Crowd surfaces retain their existing presentation and permissions.
